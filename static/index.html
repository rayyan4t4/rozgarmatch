How RozgarMatch works

1. Add your CV. Paste the text or upload a PDF — nothing is stored.

2. Get ranked matches. Every live tech listing is scored against your CV: 65% skill coverage, 35% TF-IDF text similarity.

3. Close the right gaps. Each match shows the skills you already have, the ones the job still asks for, and your top gaps across all matches — so you learn what the market actually wants.

Frequently asked questions

How does RozgarMatch match my CV to jobs?
It extracts skills from your CV using a 70+ skill taxonomy and scores every live listing by skill coverage (65%) and TF-IDF text similarity (35%), so each job is ranked by how well your actual CV fits it.

Is RozgarMatch free?
Yes — completely. Paste or upload your CV and get ranked matches, missing skills per job, and your top skill gaps.

Where do the job listings come from?
Live from the RemoteOK and Arbeitnow public APIs, filtered to tech roles and deduplicated — strong for fresh graduates targeting remote tech work.
