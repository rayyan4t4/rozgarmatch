About RozgarMatch

RozgarMatch is a free tool that matches your CV against live remote tech job listings, ranks them by how well they actually fit, and shows you the skills each job still asks for — plus the gaps worth closing first. It was built for fresh graduates who are tired of applying blind.

Who built it

RozgarMatch was designed and built by Rayyan Khan, a final-year BS Computer Science student at UET Mardan, Pakistan, and AI/ML Team Lead at ITSimplera Solutions. His work focuses on applied machine learning and generative AI — RozgarMatch grew out of watching graduates (himself included) send hundreds of applications with no feedback loop at all.

Find him on GitHub, LinkedIn, and Instagram, or read the RozgarMatch blog for guides on CV matching and skill gaps.

Where the data comes from

Listings are pulled live from the RemoteOK and Arbeitnow public APIs, filtered to tech roles and deduplicated. Matching uses a 70+ skill taxonomy and a transparent 65/35 fit score — the full method is explained on the how it works page. No CVs are stored: your text is processed to produce your matches and nothing else.
