How the CV matcher scores you

The matcher extracts skills from your CV using a taxonomy of 70+ technical skills, then scores every live listing: 65% skill coverage (weighted so jobs that specify more skills count more) and 35% TF-IDF text similarity between your CV and the job description. The full method is on the how it works page, and our step-by-step guide shows how to improve your match honestly.

Nothing is stored — your CV text is processed to produce your matches and then discarded.

CV matcher questions

What is a CV matcher?
A tool that compares your resume against job requirements and scores the fit. RozgarMatch ranks live jobs by that score and shows the evidence: matched skills and missing skills per job.

Is this resume matcher free?
Yes — completely free, no account, no sign-up.

Can I upload my CV as a PDF?
Yes — paste the text or upload a PDF or TXT file.
